## Brand & Style

This design system channels an open, modern travel experience anchored in the rich biodiversity of Bangladesh—from the Sundarbans mangrove forests to the waters of Cox's Bazar and the hill tracts of Bandarban—while scaling effortlessly for premier international exploration. The visual mood is crisp, airy, and luminous, prioritizing breathing room, high-clarity typography, and crisp atmospheric accents.

### Design Movement
The system balances **Modern High-Clarity Minimalism** with refined **Glassmorphism**:
- Generous white and pale sand canvases to evoke ocean air and open skies.
- Delicate frosted glass floating navigation and detail badges layered over vivid travel imagery.
- Balanced structural alignment inspired by cartography and navigation instruments, maintaining elegance without corporate rigidity.

### Target Audience & Emotional Impact
Designed for curious, discerning explorers, corporate group planners, and experiential travel seekers. The UI must feel reliable, calm, deeply inspiring, and friction-free—instilling confidence from trip discovery to flight and tour execution.

## Colors

The color palette reflects natural coastal, riverine, and jungle terrains under open daylight:

- **Primary (`#0D7A5F`)**: Rich Mangrove Emerald. Represents growth, exploration, and the signature identity of regional expeditions. Used for primary CTAs, active indicators, and high-priority booking milestones.
- **Secondary (`#00415A`)**: Deep Ocean Teal. Conveys institutional trust, navigational stability, and depth. Applied to structural headers, primary typography emphasis, and dark surface accents.
- **Tertiary (`#0284C7`)**: Sunlit Sky Cyan. Evokes horizon expanses and clear flight paths. Reserved for dynamic badges, progress indicators, interactive highlights, and secondary chips.
- **Neutral (`#1E293B`)**: Marine Slate. Delivers crisp legibility without harsh contrast against clean surfaces.
- **Supporting Accents**:
  - `Sunlit Amber` (`#F59E0B`): Used selectively for rating badges, seasonal offers, and urgent status alerts.
  - `Surface Pristine` (`#FAFCFB`): An ultra-clean base with faint cool undertones.
  - `Surface Glass` (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.72)`): Semi-translucent backing for floating bars and overlay chips.

## Typography

The type hierarchy relies entirely on **Plus Jakarta Sans** for its friendly geometry, open apertures, and crisp readability on screens of all sizes. 

- **Display & Headings**: Styled with tighter tracking (`-0.03em` to `-0.01em`) and bold weights to project modern editorial momentum without becoming overly heavy.
- **Body & Paragraphs**: Balanced with comfortable leading (`1.5` to `1.6`) to enable smooth reading of destination guides, tour inclusions, and itinerary breakdowns.
- **Badges & Microcopy**: Uses uppercase or tracked semi-bold tokens (`label-sm`, `label-md`) for departure stamps, booking category pills, and price tags.
- **Multilingual Support**: When pairing with Bengali script for regional collateral, set matching line-height increases of `10-15%` to allow full clearance for ascenders and diacritics.

## Layout & Spacing

A fluid grid architecture guarantees responsiveness across all consumer touchpoints:

- **Desktop (1024px+)**: 12-column fluid grid, `3rem` section margins, `1.5rem` column gutters, and a maximum content bounding box of `1280px`.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px)**: 8-column layout, `2rem` page margins, `1.25rem` gutters.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column layout, `1.25rem` outer margins, `1rem` gutters. Horizontal scrolling carousels break out cleanly into the viewport padding.

Vertical rhythm is governed by an 8pt modular cadence (`0.5rem` / `8px` increments). Section spacing leans generous (`space-xl` and higher) to evoke expansive coastlines and open horizon vistas.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy combines soft, colored ambient shadows and frosted surface layers rather than heavy drop shadows:

- **Level 0 (Flat Surface)**: Pure background tone (`#FAFCFB`) or subtle tinted off-white (`#F0F6F5`).
- **Level 1 (Card Default)**: `box-shadow: 0 4px 20px -2px rgba(0, 65, 90, 0.05), 0 1px 3px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.02)`. Borders use a translucent stroke: `1px solid rgba(13, 122, 95, 0.08)`.
- **Level 2 (Hover & Focus States)**: `box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -4px rgba(0, 65, 90, 0.12), 0 4px 8px -2px rgba(13, 122, 95, 0.04)`. Accompanied by a subtle `-2px` Y-axis lift.
- **Level 3 (Floating Bars & Modals)**: `box-shadow: 0 20px 48px -8px rgba(11, 37, 69, 0.16)`.
- **Glassmorphism Standard**: Applied to sticky headers, hero floating pills, and image overlay labels:
  - `background: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.75)`
  - `backdrop-filter: blur(12px) saturate(160%)`
  - `border: 1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.6)`

## Shapes

The design system incorporates a **Level 2 (Rounded)** shape strategy to preserve a balanced, friendly, yet architecturally crisp aesthetic:

- **Base Radius (0.5rem / 8px)**: Input controls, dropdowns, utility chips, and inline action bars.
- **Large Radius (`rounded-lg`, 1rem / 16px)**: Destination destination cards, modal sheets, itinerary timeline blocks, and ticket preview pods.
- **Pill Radius (Full / 9999px)**: Category tags (e.g., "Student Tour", "Ship Ticket"), floating hotline pill, status triggers, and primary conversion buttons.
- **Media Shapes**: Destination imagery employs gentle corner radii paired with subtle inner borders (`box-shadow: inset 0 0 0 1px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`) to maintain silhouette clarity over bright photography.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action**: Gradient fill from `#0D7A5F` to `#0A634D`, crisp white text, pill shape (`9999px` radius), `padding: 0.75rem 1.75rem`. Hover state introduces an emerald glow (`0 8px 20px rgba(13, 122, 95, 0.3)`) and smooth 150ms scale transition.
- **Secondary Action**: Translucent ocean teal tint (`rgba(0, 65, 90, 0.08)`), text `#00415A`, border `1px solid rgba(0, 65, 90, 0.15)`. Hover fills to solid `#00415A` with white typography.
- **Glass Floating Pill**: Used for quick call contacts and fast booking widgets (`01627444330 / BOOK NOW`). Frosted white background with high blur, vibrant emerald or sky-blue micro-icons, and dark slate labeling.

### Chips & Filters
- **Service Categories**: Compact pills showcasing transit modes (Corporate, Custom, Student, Ship, Air, Bus, Hotel). Unselected states feature a white base with `rgba(0, 65, 90, 0.1)` border. Selected states use solid `#00415A` or `#0D7A5F` with crisp white text and a faint glowing badge indicator.

### Tour Cards & Destination Displays
- **Structured Card**: Composed with a 16:10 or 4:3 high-resolution photography viewport, `1rem` corner rounding, floating category badge at the top-left, and transparent gradient overlays at the base.
- **Pricing & Detail Shelf**: Positioned below the media with clear font size disparity—highlighting package cost in `#0D7A5F` (`headline-sm`), duration tags in muted slate, and direct CTA arrow links.

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- **Booking Flight/Hotel Searchers**: Segmented surface containers sitting on white card beds. Clean dividers between "From", "To", "Date", and "Travelers". Focused fields show a 2px outer ring in `#0284C7` with zero layout jitter.
- **Form Controls**: Checkboxes and radio buttons use `#0D7A5F` when active, with smooth inward scale-bounce animation.

### Itinerary Timeline
- Interactive step-nodes joined by a dashed riverine path (`#0284C7`). Completed nodes display solid emerald ticks; active stops present an ambient pulsing beacon.
