The Traverse BD Story

Traverse BD began in 2017 with three backpackers and a solitary dream: to dismantle the hurdles of navigating Bangladesh’s secluded wonderlands. We saw travelers struggling with unreliable transport, opaque pricing, and fragmented local networks.

Today, we stand as the standard for eco-certified tours—combining rugged exploration with five-star safety protocols and deep-rooted Bengali hospitality.
